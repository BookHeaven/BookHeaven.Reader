> [!WARNING]
> This release requires Server v1.0.3 or higher.

## Features
- New "for you" section on the Server tab with personalized recommendations based on the books you are currently reading.

## Improvements
- The reader will now keep track of your reading history. This means that you will be able to reset your progress on a book and start over without losing your previous reading data. Only finished readings will be recorded though, so if you reset your progress on a book that you haven't finished yet, your reading history will not be affected.
- When syncing your progress to the server, your reading history will be synced as well.
